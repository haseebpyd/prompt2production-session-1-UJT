Chapter 3: Prompt Engineering / Video Generation

3e. Video Generation by Prompting

Narrative, camera, and audio layers for Google Veo 3 — plan for ~8 seconds.

Source: Panaversity — Video Generation · Tool: Google Veo 3

Core Principles

Narrative structure

Beginning → action → resolution in one clip

Audio layers

Ambient sound, dialogue, music cues

Camera movement

Dolly, pan, tracking — one move per shot

8-second planning

One clear beat; don't overload the timeline

Prompt Anatomy

  1. Scene setting
  2. Subject description
  3. Action / movement
  4. Camera angle & motion
  5. Lighting & time of day
  6. Audio (ambient, dialogue, music)
  7. Style & mood

Example: Corporate Brand Video

Modern office, golden hour. Team at glass table, analytics on screens.

Slow dolly-in. Warm window light. Collaborative gestures.

Ambient office + subtle corporate music. Cinematic 4K, 8 sec.

Best Practices & Quality Checklist

  • One primary action per clip — complexity causes artifacts
  • Specify camera motion explicitly (static vs dolly vs pan)
  • Describe lighting and time of day for consistency
  • Include audio cues — Veo 3 generates synchronized sound
  • Iterate: change one element (camera, then lighting, then action)
Before generating: Scene clear? Subject defined? One camera move? Audio specified? Under 8 seconds of story?
