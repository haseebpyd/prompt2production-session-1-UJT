Chapter 3: Prompt Engineering / UX Design

3c. UX Design by Prompting

Treat prompts like a mini design spec — clarity drives output quality in UXPilot.

Source: Panaversity — UX Design

Tool: UXPilot

UXPilot is an AI design tool (Figma/FigJam plugin) that generates production-ready UI from structured prompts. Prompt precision directly influences design quality.

One-shot mode

Rapid ideation — broad brief, explore layouts fast. Good for brainstorming.

Step-by-step mode

Detailed brief with brand colors, metrics, sample data — pixel-level control.

Key Insight

The quality of your prompt directly influences the quality of your design. Include:

  • Users — who uses this screen?
  • Metrics / data — what numbers or KPIs to show?
  • Layout — sidebar, cards, tables, charts?
  • Brand — colors, typography, tone
  • Visual references — wireframes or competitor screenshots

Example: Operations Dashboard Brief

Design a quick-commerce operations dashboard for store managers.

Users: Ops managers, 50+ locations.
Layout: Sidebar + KPI cards + alert table + date filter.
Brand: Purple #5B21B6, white cards, sans-serif.
Include realistic sample data. Mobile-responsive.

Advanced: UXPilot FigJam plugin AI Tools → Custom Prompt does not consume tokens — ideal for early ideation.
